Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Purpose

Personal Reader Sync is a personal OAuth configuration used with Hoshi Reader for reading-progress synchronization through the Google Drive API. This is not an official Hoshi Reader or Google service.

Google user data

With the user's authorization, the reader app can access and manage Google Drive files required for its synchronization feature. The intended access permission is the Google Drive drive.file scope. The purpose is to save and restore reading progress and related synchronization data in the user's Google Drive. Access is limited to the permissions the user grants.

Collection, use, storage and sharing

This public information website does not request Google sign-in, receive OAuth tokens, or collect Google Drive contents or reading progress. The synchronization functionality operates in the reader app and uses the user's Google Drive account. This website does not operate a synchronization server or independently store or share the user's Google data. Google Drive data remains subject to Google's terms and privacy policy, and the reader app's handling of local data is governed by its own behavior and policies.

User control

The user can revoke the OAuth app's permission in their Google Account's third-party access settings and can remove synchronization files from Google Drive. Revoking authorization stops future access through the revoked authorization.

Contact

For inquiries about this personal OAuth configuration, use the support email address listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
